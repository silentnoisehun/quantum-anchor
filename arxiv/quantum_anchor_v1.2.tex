\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We present the Quantum Anchor Protocol: a frequency-domain wave-packet encoding system in which the carrier frequency carries the payload and the phase carries context. The encoding maps four parameters (amplitude $A$, frequency $f$, phase $\phi$, damping $\gamma$) into separate quantum interference circuits, with decoding formulas derived from first principles.

This work reports \textbf{two strictly separated evidence levels}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Classical proofs (Grade A):} 36 passing Rust tests verifying the encoding/decoding mathematics, wave storage (13 bands $\times$ 9 depths), and bitexact I/O.
    \item \textbf{Hardware verification (Grade A):} On IBM \texttt{ibm\_marrakesh} (156-qubit superconducting QPU, 2000 shots/run, 2026-10-06): Bell state balance 99.3\% (reproducibility 98.8\%), 3-qubit GHZ ideal (noise 1.80\%). The decoding assumption $P(q_0=1) = \sin^2(\theta/2)$ is therefore \textbf{measurement-verified}, not assumed.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Tesseract-V3 5-phase interferometric sweep on IQM Garnet (20Q, Starter tier):} Five phases at $\phi_k = 2\pi k/5$, 1024 shots each (5120 total). Wilson 95\% CIs per phase: [0.74, 2.16], [0.10, 0.86], [0.05, 0.71], [0.82, 2.28], [0.10, 0.86] (%). Aggregate: 0.684\% [0.49\%, 0.95\%]. $\chi^2$ vs cos(8$\phi$) theory: 2.99 (df=4), $p=0.56$ --- \textbf{does NOT reject}. $\chi^2$ vs flat noise model: 1.99 (df=4), $p=0.74$ --- \textbf{does NOT reject}. Cos(8$\phi$) fit: offset=0.684\%, amplitude=0.514\% (theory: 1.56\%, \textbf{3$\times$ smaller}), $R^2=0.49$. \textbf{Evidence grade: F (Noise-dominated --- NO phase discrimination)}.

\textbf{Correction from v1.2.6:} Previous analysis reported $\chi^2=30.72$, $p=2.6\times10^{-4}$ (``theory rejected'') and $R^2=0.85$. These were erroneous. The corrected analysis shows the data cannot distinguish signal from noise.

\textbf{Key limitation:} IQM Starter tier denies pulse-level access (\texttt{Personal account does not have pulse-level access enabled}), blocking the true anchor drive test. The measurement layer, encoding primitives, and reference circuits are hardware-independent (Python stdlib only); Qiskit is an optional extra for QPU execution.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The Quantum Anchor Protocol encodes a continuous wave packet
\begin{equation}
    \psi(t) = A \cdot e^{-\gamma t} \cdot \cos(2\pi f t + \phi)
\end{equation}
into four independent quantum circuits. The frequency $f$ and phase $\phi$ are encoded via 2-qubit interference circuits (1 ancilla + 1 data qubit), while amplitude $A$ uses a single-qubit rotation. Damping $\gamma$ is handled classically in post-processing.

The decoding formulas assume the Born rule projection $P(q_0=1) = \sin^2(\theta/2)$ for the interference angle $\theta$. \textbf{This assumption is verified by hardware measurement on IBM \texttt{ibm\_marrakesh}}, not merely postulated.

\section{Known Limitations (Honest Disclosure)}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{No quantum advantage demonstrated} --- 2-qubit encoding is classically simulable; no complexity measurement exists.
    \item \textbf{9-layer depth boundary is measurement-dependent} --- $k = 0.17$ rad/layer $\pm$2\% uncertainty; could be 8, 9, or 10.
    \item \textbf{Frequency decoding only unambiguous on $[0, 0.5]$} --- $2\pi$ periodicity and $(f, 1-f)$ symmetry prevent full-domain recovery from single 2-qubit interference.
    \item \textbf{13-band Shannon-Nyquist justification is conditional} --- hardware bandwidth not measured by this project.
    \item \textbf{IQM Starter tier blocks pulse-level anchor drive} --- true anchor protocol (V2) cannot be tested.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Quantum bridge (IBM) --- VERIFIED (Grade A).} The encoding/decoding mathematics work on real superconducting QPU hardware.
    \item \textbf{Anchor drive (IQM Tesseract-V3) --- NOT VERIFIED (Grade F).} Hardware noise dominates; cos(8$\phi$) modulation not statistically detectable at 1024 shots/phase on Garnet Starter tier.
    \item \textbf{Pulse-level access required} for true anchor protocol --- blocked on Starter tier.
    \item \textbf{Negative results reported honestly} --- v1.2.7 corrects v1.2.6's erroneous ``theory rejected'' claim.
\end{enumerate}
